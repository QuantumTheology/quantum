    Part 4 · Christological Proof

    The 4-Step Case That the Savior Must Be God and Man

    1

    Sin against an infinitely holy God creates a moral debt too large for any creature to pay.

    2

    Humans, angels and prophets are finite. None has the merit to cover a debt against the infinite.

    3

    A good judge doesn't ignore wrong, yet God loves sinners completely. Justice and mercy both have to be satisfied.

    4

    Therefore, the Savior must be fully man (to stand in our place, Hebrews 2:14–17) and fully God (to bear it, John 1:1; 8:58; 10:30), and His bodily resurrection confirms it (Axiom 17). Honest note: this is the coherence of the gospel, not a stand-alone proof.

    Part 5 · Formal Thesis

    Why Atheism Fails

    Thesis statement

    Atheism, the view that only physical matter and energy exist with no transcendent Creator, has to pay six debts it can't pay: why anything exists, why it began, why it is tuned for life, where the code of life came from, how minds and reason arise, and what grounds objective morality. Each has a price, and S-GUOT lists them.

    Debt 1 and 2

    Existence & Beginning

    The universe is contingent (Axiom 1), and three independent routes (BGV, thermodynamics, Hilbert's Hotel) point to a beginning (Axiom 2). The cause is not made of space, time or matter.

    Debt 3 and 4

    Tuning & Code

    The constants are dialed to allow life (Axiom 3), and the first cell needed a code plus a decoder, the one thing natural selection can't explain since it starts after replication (Axiom 5).

    Debt 5

    Mind & Reason

    Experience, aboutness and reason are hard to derive from mindless matter (Axioms 6–8), and Plantinga's EAAN says naturalism plus unguided evolution undercuts our trust in the very reasoning that led to it.

    Debt 6

    Morality

    Atheists can be good, but they can't ground why anything is binding or why a person is worth more than a pear (Axioms 9–10). Don't say atheists can't be moral. Say atheism can't ground morality.

    What Now?

    Every worldview fails. Jesus doesn't.

    If Jesus really rose from the dead, He isn't just an argument to win. He's a Person to know. Here's how.

    1

    You've sinned against a holy God

    Everyone has broken God's law: lying, lust, pride, hate (Romans 3:23). Sin against an infinitely holy God creates a debt you can't pay.

    2

    Sin earns death, but God offers a gift

    “The wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 6:23).

    3

    Jesus paid your debt in full

    The fully God, fully Man Savior died in your place and rose again (Romans 5:8; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4). God made Him who knew no sin to be sin for us (2 Corinthians 5:21).

    4

    It's grace through faith, not works

    You can't earn it with rituals, rules or good deeds (Ephesians 2:8–9; Titus 3:5). No priest, saint or church can stand between you and God. There is one Mediator, Christ Jesus (1 Timothy 2:5).

    5

    Turn and trust

    Turn from your sin and trust Jesus alone as your Lord and Savior: “If you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved” (Romans 10:9; Acts 3:19; John 1:12).

    Talk to God

    Not a magic formula. God sees your heart.

    Words don't save you. Jesus does. But if you're ready to trust Him, you can pray something like this:

    “Jesus, I know I've sinned against You and I can't save myself. I believe You died for my sins and rose from the dead. I turn from my sin and trust You alone to save me. Be my Lord and my God. Amen.”
    Next steps

    Start following Jesus

    • Read the Bible. Start with the Gospel of John (the reader is above).
    • Pray every day. You have direct access to God through Jesus (Hebrews 4:16).
    • Get baptized. A public declaration that you belong to Jesus (Matthew 28:19).
    • Find a Bible-teaching church. You weren't made to follow Jesus alone (Hebrews 10:24–25).
